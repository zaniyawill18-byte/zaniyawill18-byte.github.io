Study 002

An image pulled apart, given depth, and allowed to become something else.

Study 002 transforms a photographic portrait into a three dimensional field of particles. Built in TouchDesigner, the work explores the space between recognition and abstraction where an image stops behaving like a surface and begins to feel like an environment.

001

Overview

2026

What happens when an image is no longer asked to stay still?

A photograph usually presents itself as a finished surface. I wanted to treat it as something less fixed, an arrangement of color and position that could be separated, displaced, and rebuilt in space.

Each pixel carries a fragment of the original image. Once converted into point data, those fragments can move beyond the flat photograph while continuing to hold its color, structure, and memory.

The finished system holds the portrait between two states. From one angle, it remains familiar. From another, it becomes color, depth, and movement. It becomes an image that can no longer be understood from a single position.

006

Reflection

2026
The image returned differently

This project taught me to treat image data as visual material. Instead of applying an effect to a finished photograph, I rebuilt the photograph as a system of points, something that could occupy space, respond to transformation, and still preserve traces of its original form.
