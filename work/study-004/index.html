Overview

2026

What if watching a film could become an act of collecting and creating?

Beyond the Credits imagines a streaming experience where collecting a visual moment never separates it from its creative lineage. The project connects inspiration, discovery, and attribution so the people behind the frame remain part of the experience.

002

The actual problem

2026

The actual problem

Visually curious viewers already screenshot scenes, identify songs, search for clothing, and save references elsewhere. That process is fragmented:

  • The moment becomes separated from the film.
  • The creative contributor is often lost.
  • Inspiration becomes a camera roll full of unexplained screenshots.
  • Streaming remains consumption rather than creative participation.
