Beyond the Credits

A streaming experience that lets viewers collect the looks, sounds, compositions, and environments that move them, then transforms those moments into a personal visual archive—with every creative contribution properly credited.

001

Overview

2026

What if watching a film could become an act of collecting and creating?

Beyond the Credits imagines a streaming experience where collecting a visual moment never separates it from its creative lineage. The project connects inspiration, discovery, and attribution so the people behind the frame remain part of the experience.

002

The problem

2026

The problem

Visually curious viewers already screenshot scenes, identify songs, search for clothing, and save references elsewhere. That process is fragmented:

  1. 01

    The moment becomes separated from the film.

  2. 02

    The creative contributor is often lost.

  3. 03

    Inspiration becomes a camera roll full of unexplained screenshots.

  4. 04

    Streaming remains consumption rather than creative participation.

004

Design approach

2026

Follow curiosity without leaving the film.

I designed Beyond the Credits around one goal: helping viewers follow their curiosity without leaving the film.

Five principles

I used five principles to guide the experience.

  1. 01Protect immersion
  2. 02Keep context attached to the moment
  3. 03Credit the full creative ecosystem
  4. 04Make collecting rewarding
  5. 05Make returning to the film effortless
Experience structure

From there, I simplified the experience into a clear structure.

  1. Film
  2. Moment
  3. Creative Lens
  4. Contributor
  5. Your Cut
Final interaction

This led to the final interaction.

  1. Collect
  2. Choose what moved you
  3. Discover who made it
  4. Save it
  5. Return to the film

Visually, I kept the interface restrained so the film imagery, contributor stories, and collected moments could carry the experience.
